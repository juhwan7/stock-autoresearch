# Source policy

## Tier 1 — primary
Government and regulator publications, exchange notices, statutory filings, company filings/IR/newsroom materials, central-bank publications, court/legal documents, official procurement or contract documents.

## Tier 2 — high-quality secondary
Major wire services and specialist publications with named sourcing and direct links to primary material.

## Tier 3 — discovery only
Aggregators, unsourced blogs, social posts, forums and reposts. These can generate a lead but cannot by themselves support a confirmed material claim.

## Claim labels
- `verified_fact`: directly supported by a strong source.
- `official_claim`: stated by an interested party or authority but not independently established.
- `analysis`: interpretation derived from evidence.
- `inference`: plausible connection that requires assumptions.
- `unverified`: insufficient evidence.

## Conflict handling
When sources disagree, record the disagreement, dates, definitions and likely reason for the difference. Do not average incompatible figures.
